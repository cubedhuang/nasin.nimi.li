# nasin.nimi.li

o lukin lon [lipu ni](https://nasin.nimi.li)!

lipu ni li poki e ilo sona mi pi toki pona.

ilo wan li lukin e toki li pana e sona ni: nimi li lon nasin seme li lon poki seme?

ilo ante li lukin e nimi li pana e sona ni: nimi seme li poka lon kon? nimi seme li weka? nimi seme li lon kulupu?

## English

[This website](https://nasin.nimi.li) houses demonstrations for several projects I've made with Toki Pona.

One tool is a parser, which takes in a Toki Pona sentence and reveals possible parse trees, ranking them by likelihood.

Another one is a word embedding visualizer.

## Papers

The demos on the **parser**, **counter**, and **charts** pages were used in my paper on [Language Change and Variation in Toki Pona](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10246). The paper is currently still a pre-print. At some point, it may appear in the peer-reviewed [UGA Working Papers in Linguistics, Vol 8](https://linguistics.uga.edu/working-papers).

The **embeddings** page is a result of my research on [Toki Pona and Word2Vec](https://aclanthology.org/2026.scil-main.4/). This paper was peer-reviewed and published in the ACL Anthology and presented at the Society for Computation in Linguistics 2026.
